THE THREAD THAT CONNECTS IT ALL

Different places.
Shared possibilities.

I’m Edlawit, a curator and video producer based in Silver Spring, Maryland.

My experience spans art talks and a seminar at the Guggenheim, daily pavilion operations at the Biennale, gallery operations at Saatchi Yates, curation at Microcosmo Gallery, and multilingual cultural mediation at Venice Design Week.

Alongside this work, I produce and edit videos with ITS. Across both practices, I’m drawn to cultural exchange and to creating space for artists and curators whose work has yet to be seen.
